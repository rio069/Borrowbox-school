BorrowBox: The Closed School Sharing Economy

"Don’t buy it. Borrow it." Eliminating \$1.2B in single-use student equipment waste while fostering campus trust.

01 • The Problem

Students Overspend on Short-Term Equipment

Every semester, students buy expensive graphing calculators (₹2,500+), reference textbooks, lab coats, and project kits used for only 2 to 3 weeks of exams. These items end up gathering dust in school lockers, parents overspend, and landfill waste skyrockets.

02 • The Solution

A Verified, Closed-Campus Sharing Platform

BorrowBox gives Authpur National Model School an internal circular economy. Only verified students with institutional emails (`@authpurmodel.edu`) can enter. Exchanges are secured using 6-digit confirmation codes at supervised campus desks (Library, Reception).

03 • 4-Step Frictionless Workflow
1. Find
Search calculators, books, lab coats in your school catalog.
2. Request
Select loan dates and send a request to classmate.
3. Exchange
Meet at Library Desk and verify 6-digit code.
4. Return
Drop off item, confirm receipt, and boost Trust Score!
04 • Business Model (B2B2C)

School Annual ESG Subscription

Schools pay an annual sustainability subscription (\$2.50 per student/year) to provide BorrowBox as a licensed campus utility. Schools receive automated ESG reports, accreditation credits, reduced locker waste, and enhanced community trust.

Closed School Verification

BorrowBox is an authenticated, internal campus network — NOT a public marketplace.

  • Domain Restricted: Only accounts ending in @authpurmodel.edu can join.
  • Supervised Drop-off Points: Items are only exchanged at staff-monitored campus desks (Library, Reception).
  • 6-Digit Exchange Verification: A temporary one-time code prevents theft and confirms physical handover.
  • Zero Cash Friction: Built entirely on community trust scores, peer accountability, and optional refundable deposits.
